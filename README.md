Privacy Policy
Version 1.0.0 — Last updated September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how AKMD Consulting (“we”, “us”) handles information in connection with the Flight Oxygen app (“the App”). We are committed to protecting your privacy and the privacy of the patients whose information you may enter into the App.

1. Information we DO NOT collect
The App does not require an account, does not log in, and does not transmit any data you enter to us or to any third party. The in-flight oxygen / HCT inputs you tap or slide stay on your device. We do not collect names, dates of birth, identifiers, clinical values, device identifiers, advertising IDs, or any analytics events tied to a specific user.

2. Information stored locally on your device
The App stores your in-progress clinical inputs (taps on disease and yes/no questions) in memory while you are using the App. When you leave the App, that working state is cleared. Your display preferences (appearance, text size) and a one-time acknowledgement of this Agreement are stored via iOS UserDefaults on your device only.

3. iOS system permissions
The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, calendar, location, HealthKit, photos, or files. Opening the BTS citation uses the system browser.

4. Children’s data
The App does not knowingly collect any information from children. Inputs that touch paediatric pathways (CF, preterm, infant, and child chronic-lung-disease notes) are entered by the clinician for educational evaluation; no patient-identifying information is captured.

5. Apple App Store receipt
Apple processes your purchase and may share with us a non-personal, anonymous App Store receipt metadata (e.g. app version, transaction type) under Apple’s standard terms. We do not link this to any individual user profile because we have none.

6. Third-party services
The App embeds a single link to the published BTS guideline on thorax.bmj.com. Tapping that link opens your default browser. Your interaction with that website is governed by BMJ’s privacy policy, not this one.

7. International transfers
Because we do not collect your data, no international data transfer occurs. Apple’s receipt metadata handling is described in Apple’s Privacy Policy.

8. Your rights
You can clear all local preferences at any time via the “Reset” button in the App or by deleting the App. Because we hold no personal data on our servers, there is nothing for us to delete, export, or correct.

9. Security
The App runs offline. Any clinical information you enter remains in volatile memory and is not written to disk. You are responsible for safeguarding the device on which the App is installed (e.g. passcode, Face ID, FileVault on paired Mac).

10. No automated decision-making with personal data
The App’s clinical logic runs entirely on your device against the inputs you provide. It is not connected to any server. It does not constitute “automated decision-making with respect to individuals” under GDPR Article 22, because no individual’s personal data is processed by us.

11. Changes to this Policy
We may update this Policy. Material changes will be communicated in-app or via an App Store update. The version and date at the top of this document will always reflect the current version.
